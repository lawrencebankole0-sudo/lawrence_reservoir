QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Select your food, add it to your basket, enter your details and click the WhatsApp order button.

Will my order go to WhatsApp?

Yes. The website creates an order message containing your order details and opens WhatsApp with the message ready to send.

Will I receive an order number?

Yes. The website automatically creates a unique order number such as LR-20260811-4821.

Can I request special instructions?

Yes. You can add instructions such as extra sauce or other preferences in the checkout form.

Can I order for pickup?

Yes. The checkout form allows customers to select Delivery, Pickup or Dine In.
